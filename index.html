Our Methodology

The Turnkey Experience

From the first sketch to white-glove handover, our structured 5-step process delivers stress-free excellence.

01

Discovery & Spatial Audit

Comprehensive site survey, lifestyle questionnaire, and spatial flow optimization.

02

3D Visualization

Photorealistic 3D architectural renders and virtual walkthroughs before fabrication starts.

03

Material Curation

Physical moodboard sampling at our design studio—marbles, woods, veneers, and brass.

04

Precision Execution

German factory modular fabrication with strict daily on-site architectural supervision.

05

Handover & Styling

Deep cleaning, designer soft-furnishing curation, and guaranteed 10-year warranty handover.
